# Third-party components

- Electron is downloaded unmodified from the official electron/electron release. The installer preserves its Electron and Chromium notices. The executable is renamed for launch; its bytes and embedded manifest are not edited.
- The pinned NSIS toolchain is downloaded from electron-userland/electron-builder-binaries. NSIS is used to build the installer; its distribution includes `COPYING`.
- Device names identify compatibility only and are the property of their respective owners. No manufacturer affiliation or endorsement is implied.
- Manufacturer drivers, APO DLLs, setup programs, background media, recordings and default PSF files are not included. Compatible audio effects use the existing system driver and APO installed by the user.
